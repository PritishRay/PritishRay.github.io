Foundations of Non-Relativistic Quantum Mechanics: Mathematical Formalism and Wave Dynamics

Authored by Pritish Burman Ray | Date: May 25, 2026

Abstract:

This paper is an introductory overview of non-relativistic Quantum Mechanics, the historical transition from classical mechanics to wave-particle duality, and written with the purpose of establishing the mathematical framework of Hilbert spaces using Dirac bra-ket notation, and analyze the fundamental postulates of quantum theory. The time-dependent and time-independent Schrödinger equations are derived and applied to standard potential configurations, illustrating the physical significance of probability distributions and quantum tunneling.
